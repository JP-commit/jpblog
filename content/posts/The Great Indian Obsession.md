# The Great Indian Obsession
The untold story of Indian Engineers

The great Indian Obsession is a book by Adithya Iyer, that I recently started reading. It is a very good book that I would recommend to every Indians, especially parents. The book contains the origin stories of many persons which led to foundation of Indian Education System, the IITs, the Coaching centers in Kota . The book also discusses about the mindset of Indian parents when it comes to their children's education and the stress and dilemma that their children face. 
The book starts with the joker's dialogue from the movie The Dark Knight - "Do I really look like a guy with a plan? You know what I am? I'am dog chasing car. I wouldn't know what to do with one if I caught it! I just... do things"
The book is fun to read and also enlightening some times. Give it try and if you had done engineering, I think it would resonate with you(At least it did with me).
